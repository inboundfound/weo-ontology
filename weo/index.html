WEO — the Web Engine Optimization ontology

Namespace https://inboundfound.github.io/weo-ontology/weo# · prefix weo: · v0.2 · repo · CC BY 4.0

One standards-grounded vocabulary for how web engines — search, generative, answer, conversational — see, cite, and serve content. Every term is typed by the kind of claim it makes; the strata run from durable bedrock to interpretive surface:

entityepisodeobservationderivationinterpretationnorm
Modules: weo-core (substrate) · weo-visibility (xEO) · weo-engagement (draft) · weo-decision (interpretation tier) · weo-strategy (norms tier) · weo-align (schema.org / PROV-O / SKOS bridges). Machine-readable Turtle: core, visibility, engagement, decision, strategy, align; JSON-LD context.

Playbook entityweo-strategy

weo:Playbook

A reusable, discipline-scoped bundle of Diagnostics, Tactics, and Practices — an 'SEO playbook', an 'AEO playbook', a 'local playbook'. Durable and named, like a Diagnostic. Its scope is the DISCIPLINE, not the account: a Playbook is authored once (by the community or an agency) and reused across every client. What varies per client or campaign is which Playbooks a tenancy activates and which Practices it overrides — never the Playbook itself.

Practice normweo-strategy

weo:Practice

A standing rule that governs how recommendations are made under stated conditions. Best-practices, SOPs, and lessons-learned are all Practices, distinguished by source, not by class. A Practice does one of three things (practiceKind): veto a candidate (guardrail), reorder it (preference), or attach a caveat (guidance). Revisable and versioned (supersedes); scoped to the concepts it appliesToConcept; audited on the recommendations it fired on (appliedPractice).

hasRole derivationweo-strategy

weo:hasRole

The structural role a URL plays on its site, as a slotted-in concept. A recommended starter scheme is coarse and purpose-based: brand-core (home, about), commercial (services, products, sales), content (articles, marketing), infrastructure (pagination, archives, tag and category indexes). Assignment is a DERIVATION from graph and crawl structure — inbound link_position, index-vs-leaf link patterns, template similarity — not URL-string matching; it carries method and confidence, and 'unknown' is a valid, conservative state. Parallels core's inTopic. Norms bind to these roles via appliesToConcept, so 'never redirect an infrastructure page for ranking' is a rule over a role, not a growing list of regexes.
